The practical bits

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a turntable or other equipment?

No. Place your subject somewhere with room to walk around it and use the guide to keep your distance and framing steady.

Does Orbit12 upload my photos?

Not automatically. Raw frames, motion readings, rendering, and project storage stay local. A finished MP4 leaves Orbit12 only when you select a destination in Apple’s system share sheet. Apple device backups are controlled separately by your system settings.

What does Orbit12 create?

A silent, six-second, 1080 × 1080 H.264 MP4 assembled from twelve evenly spaced photos.

Can I fix a missed frame?

Yes. Review every angle and retake any individual frame before creating the final loop.

Can deleted projects be recovered?

Orbit12 has no server or account-based restore. Deleting a project removes its active local files; any prior Apple device backup is governed by your system backup settings.
