The practical bits

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a turntable or other equipment?

No. Place your subject somewhere with room to walk around it and use the guide to keep your distance and framing steady.

Does Orbit12 upload my photos or video?

No automatic upload occurs. Video, motion data, scan images, processing, and project storage stay local. An MP4 or USDZ leaves Orbit12 only when you select a destination in Apple’s system share sheet. Apple device backups are controlled separately by your system settings.

What does Quick Orbit create?

A silent, six-second, 1080 × 1080 H.264 MP4 at 30 fps. Twelve extracted keyframes are saved as review thumbnails, not used as the video frame rate.

Can my iPhone create a 3D model?

Only when Apple Object Capture and on-device photogrammetry report support on a LiDAR-equipped iPhone. Standard non-LiDAR models such as iPhone 16 keep Quick Orbit but cannot start 3D Scan.

What happens to my older Orbit12 projects?

Existing legacy twelve-frame projects remain in the local library and can still be reopened and reviewed.

Can deleted projects be recovered?

Orbit12 has no server or account-based restore. Deleting a project removes its active local files; any prior Apple device backup is governed by your system backup settings.
