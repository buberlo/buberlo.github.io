Orbit12

Walk once. Spin anything. Orbit12 turns twelve motion-spaced iPhone photos into a six-second square loop.

Quick help

  • Keep the object centered and your distance steady.
  • Move slowly in either direction and let each haptic tick confirm a frame.
  • Review or retake any angle before creating the final loop.
  • Use the share button to save or send the finished MP4 with the iOS share sheet.

Your projects

Projects live only inside Orbit12 on your iPhone. Delete an orbit from its project card, or remove the app to erase all Orbit12 projects. Orbit12 has no account or cloud restore.
