Privacy Policy

Orbit12 is designed to work without collecting your data.

What Orbit12 collects

Orbit12 does not collect, transmit, sell, or use personal data for tracking. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, backend service, or runtime artificial intelligence.

Camera and motion

With your permission, Orbit12 uses the rear camera to capture twelve object photos and device motion to space those photos around your walk. This processing happens on your iPhone. Orbit12 does not upload camera frames or motion readings.

Local storage

Source frames, project metadata, and finished videos are stored inside the Orbit12 app container on your iPhone. They remain there until you delete the project or uninstall Orbit12. Because there is no Orbit12 account or server, deleted projects cannot be restored by us.

Sharing

Orbit12 shares a finished video only when you choose the share button. Apple’s system share sheet then lets you select a destination. The privacy practices of that destination apply after you choose it.

Children

Orbit12 does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If Orbit12’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before the change is released. Questions can be sent to kern.konrad@gmail.com.
