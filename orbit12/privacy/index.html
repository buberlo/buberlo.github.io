Privacy policy

Your work stays
in your control.

Orbit12 is designed to work without collecting your data.

Effective July 17, 2026

At a glance

  • No account
  • No automatic upload
  • No analytics
  • No advertising
  • No tracking
  • No runtime AI

01

What Orbit12 collects

Orbit12 does not collect, sell, or use personal data for tracking. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, developer-operated backend service, or runtime artificial intelligence.

02

Camera and motion

With your permission, Orbit12 uses the rear camera to capture twelve object photos and device motion to space those photos around your walk. This processing happens on your iPhone. Orbit12 does not automatically upload raw camera frames or motion readings.

03

Local storage

Source frames, project metadata, and finished videos are stored locally inside the Orbit12 app container. Orbit12 has no developer-operated upload service. Depending on your device settings, Apple system backups may include app data; those backups are controlled by Apple and your system backup settings. Because Orbit12 has no account or server, we cannot restore a deleted project.

04

Sharing

A finished MP4 leaves Orbit12 only when you choose the share button and select a destination in Apple’s system share sheet. The privacy practices of that destination apply after you choose it. Raw frames, motion readings, and project metadata are not passed to the share destination.

05

Children

Orbit12 does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

06

Changes and contact

If Orbit12’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before the change is released.

Privacy questions can be sent to kern.konrad@gmail.com.
