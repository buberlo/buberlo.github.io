Get out of bed! — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-07-27

Get out of bed! collects no data. None.

The app has no networking capability: no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, no third-party SDKs, no crash reporting, no tracking of any kind. Everything the game does happens on your device and stays there.

  • Data collected: none.
  • Data shared: none — there is nothing to share.
  • Data sold: none — there is nothing to sell.
  • Permissions requested: none.

If a future update ever changes any of this, this policy will be updated first, and the App Store privacy label with it.
