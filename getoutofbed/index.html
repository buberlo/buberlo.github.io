GET OUT OF BED!

a morning routine. if it really is morning. — support

How to play

  • Left thumb: move. Drag anywhere else: look. USE: interact with what you're facing.
  • Finish your morning routine — alarm, curtains, teeth, kettle. The front door stays locked until it's done.
  • Then decide: if this morning is real, walk out the front door. If it's a nightmare, get back into bed. Three correct calls in a row and you're free.
  • Real mornings are boringly identical. Sameness is the tell.
  • The peephole shows the landing outside your door. Checking it costs nothing. Trusting it might.

FAQ

The game is dark. It's supposed to be — but play with the room lights low and phone brightness at normal. Sound on: one of the anomalies is audio-only.

Does it save my progress? Each run is short by design (a full win takes a few minutes). Closing the app ends the run — the flat doesn't wait for you.

Nothing collects my data, right? Right. No network, no analytics, no accounts. See the privacy policy.
