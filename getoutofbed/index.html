GET OUT OF BED!

a morning routine. if it really is morning. — support

How to play

  • Left thumb: move. Drag anywhere else: look. USE: interact with what you're facing.
  • Finish your morning routine — alarm, curtains, teeth, kettle. The front door stays locked until it's done.
  • Use the unlocked door once to inspect the threshold. A lit staircase or the void stays visible while your controls remain active. Use the open doorway again to step out, or turn back to bed.
  • Make three correct calls in a row. The winning call must be stepping through a real doorway; sleeping on the third correct call holds the streak until you leave on a later real morning.
  • Real mornings are boringly identical. Sameness is the tell.
  • The peephole shows the landing outside your door. Checking it costs nothing. Trusting it might.
  • Visual Audio Cues in Options adds faint corner static for whispers or backward clock ticks. It is off by default and intentionally makes those audio anomalies easier to detect.
  • Reduce Flashing replaces the red-kitchen flicker with a slow pulse and abrupt failure cuts with slow fades. It is off by default.
  • Display Calibration appears once on first launch and can be reopened from Options at any time.

FAQ

The game is dark. It's supposed to be, but the painting should remain barely visible after Display Calibration. Reopen Calibrate Display in Options if the room or screen changes. Some anomalies are audio-only unless you enable Visual Audio Cues.

Does it save my progress? Local records, totals, and Options are saved. Your active morning and streak are not; closing the app ends the current run.

Nothing collects my data, right? Right. No network, no analytics, no accounts. See the privacy policy.
